I worked across solution architecture, document extraction/OCR, validation, database integration, the Streamlit interface, testing, and technical documentation. These were shared responsibilities with teammates rather than exclusive authorship of individual modules.

The original implementation was published in [cjm010/AiOcr](https://github.com/cjm010/AiOcr). Its upload and commit history should not be treated as a complete allocation of the team's offline contributions.

## Portfolio additions

This branch adds seven labeled synthetic invoices; a local benchmark for eight fields; separate extraction, validation, and timing results; tests for scoring and isolation; and architecture and demonstration guides. These additions were prepared with AI coding assistance. The original team's code and history remain intact.

## Evidence and claims

Results apply only to the checked-in synthetic text corpus. They do not substantiate workplace accuracy, cost, or productivity percentages, and they do not measure OCR or multi-agent/RAG performance. Present this as a team capstone unless separate employment context is established.
